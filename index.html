Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Getting started
What is a SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) allows you to invest a fixed amount periodically in an eligible mutual fund scheme, helping you invest with discipline over the long term.

Can I start with a small amount?

Yes. Many mutual fund schemes offer SIPs with relatively small minimum investment amounts, subject to scheme terms.

Can I invest a lump sum?

Yes. You can make lump-sum investments in eligible mutual fund schemes, subject to the applicable scheme terms.

I already have SIPs. Can you review my portfolio?

Yes. We can help you organise and understand your existing investments, review your portfolio and discuss available investment options.

How do I choose the right investment?

The suitable option depends on factors such as your financial goals, investment horizon, risk profile and liquidity requirements. We help you understand the available product options.

Can I invest for specific financial goals?

Yes. Investments can be planned around goals such as children's education, retirement, wealth creation, property purchase and other long-term objectives.

How can I get started?

Simply contact us and share your investment objective. We can help you understand the available solutions and the next steps.

Products & portfolio
Do you provide insurance solutions?

Yes. We assist with life and general insurance solutions, subject to product availability, eligibility and applicable terms.

What is portfolio diversification?

Diversification means spreading investments across different asset classes, categories or securities to help manage concentration risk.

What is a Portfolio Management Service (PMS)?

PMS is a professionally managed investment service where an eligible investor's portfolio is managed according to the applicable mandate and strategy.

What is a Specialised Investment Fund (SIF)?

SIFs are specialised investment products with specific investment strategies and eligibility requirements. The applicable minimum investment and regulatory conditions depend on the product.

What is an Alternative Investment Fund (AIF)?

AIFs are investment vehicles that invest in alternative asset classes or strategies and are generally available to investors meeting specified eligibility requirements.

Can I invest in PMS, SIF or AIF?

Eligibility depends on the product, applicable regulations and minimum investment requirements. We can help you understand the available options and eligibility criteria.

Can I switch my existing mutual fund investment?

Switching may be possible between eligible schemes, subject to the respective scheme terms, applicable taxes and other conditions.

How often should I review my portfolio?

A portfolio review can be considered periodically and whenever there is a significant change in your financial goals, income, risk profile or investment horizon.

Risk & our role
Are mutual fund returns guaranteed?

No. Mutual fund and market-linked investments are subject to market risks. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

Do you guarantee investment returns?

No. We do not guarantee returns on market-linked investments. Investment outcomes depend on market conditions and the performance of the underlying investments.

What is the role of Growth Guru?

Growth Guru is a financial product distributor. We help clients understand available products, complete the investment process and provide ongoing service. We are not investment advisers. We do not charge a fee for advice and earn commission from the product manufacturers on products distributed.
